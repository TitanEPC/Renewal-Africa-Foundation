About Us

Renewal Africa Foundation

A Kenyan foundation working alongside communities to restore land and water, build resilient livelihoods, and protect the people most often left behind.

Our vision

A renewed Africa, rooted in resilient communities.

We believe environmental restoration and human dignity are inseparable. Renewal Africa Foundation exists because the health of the land and the health of a community rise and fall together — you cannot restore one without the other.

We work where need is greatest: with smallholder farmers, women, youth, and refugees; with prisoners and ex-convicts rebuilding their lives; with the elderly, persons with disabilities, and the homeless — treating each as a partner in renewal, not a recipient of charity.

Our mission

Integrated, community-led, built to last.

We enhance the well-being of marginalized communities across Kenya by restoring ecosystems, expanding access to clean water and sanitation, growing clean energy use, strengthening climate-smart farming, and improving health — as four connected programmes, not separate projects.

Every intervention is designed to reinforce the others: a rehabilitated water point supports both health and agriculture; a restored watershed supports both climate resilience and livelihoods.

How the board works

Structure & oversight

Our board is deliberately small — between five and nine members — so decisions stay close to the work. Three members hold the Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer roles, meeting regularly in person or remotely to review programme progress and finances.

Theme 01 of 04

WASH, Health, Hygiene & Community Resilience

Increasing access to safe water, sanitation, and hygiene — including in prison settings — while advancing preventive health and community resilience.

01 · WASH & Health 02 · Climate & Energy 03 · Agriculture 04 · Protection

What we do

We work with counties, prison facilities, and vulnerable communities to rehabilitate water infrastructure, install sanitation facilities, and run hygiene-behaviour programmes — pairing infrastructure with preventive-health messaging so gains are sustained.

  • Borehole and water-point rehabilitation
  • Sanitation facility construction, including in detention settings
  • Community hygiene promotion and behaviour-change campaigns
  • Preventive health outreach and referral linkages
Featured project

Kitui water point rehabilitation

We rehabilitated a community borehole serving roughly 1,200 residents, paired with a hygiene-promotion campaign delivered through partner schools.

Theme 02 of 04

Environment, Climate Resilience & Clean Energy

Ecosystem restoration, sustainable natural resource management, and adoption of clean and renewable energy.

01 · WASH & Health 02 · Climate & Energy 03 · Agriculture 04 · Protection

What we do

We support reforestation, catchment protection, and community-based natural resource management, alongside the adoption of clean cookstoves, solar lighting, and other renewable energy solutions that reduce both emissions and household costs.

  • Tree-planting and ecosystem restoration
  • Community natural resource management plans
  • Clean cookstove and solar energy distribution
  • Climate resilience training for local leaders
Featured project

Kajiado catchment restoration

We established a community tree nursery and catchment-protection programme in partnership with a local women's group.

Theme 03 of 04

Sustainable Agriculture & Livelihood Development

Climate-smart agriculture, food security, and livelihood opportunities for smallholder farmers, women, youth, and refugees.

01 · WASH & Health 02 · Climate & Energy 03 · Agriculture 04 · Protection

What we do

We train smallholder farmers in climate-smart techniques, support access to markets and inputs, and design livelihood pathways tailored to women, youth, and refugee communities.

  • Climate-smart agriculture training
  • Farmer group formation and market linkages
  • Livelihood diversification for women and youth
  • Support for refugee and host-community livelihoods
Featured project

Machakos smallholder training cohort

We delivered a season-long climate-smart agriculture training cohort for smallholder farmers, combined with input support and market linkages.

Theme 04 of 04

Protection, Care & Social Inclusion

Social protection, direct care, and community-based support for the most vulnerable.

01 · WASH & Health 02 · Climate & Energy 03 · Agriculture 04 · Protection

What we do

We provide direct care and community-based support that restores dignity and inclusion for prisoners and ex-convicts, the elderly, persons with disabilities, homeless individuals, and the impoverished.

  • Reintegration support for ex-convicts
  • Elderly and disability-inclusive community care
  • Direct support for homeless and impoverished individuals
  • Referral and advocacy with local authorities
Featured project

Nairobi reintegration circle

We run a peer-support and livelihood reintegration circle for formerly incarcerated individuals returning to their communities.
